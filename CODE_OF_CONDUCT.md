# Code of Conduct

English | [简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md)

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct, and the original text is authoritative. Below is a short summary.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, and respect different viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept criticism of the work, not the person.
- Focus on what is best for the community.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, insults, or discriminatory remarks.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as an email address, QQ number, or home address, without their consent.
- Other conduct that would clearly be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to this project's issues, PRs, and discussions, and to public spaces where someone is representing the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

To report something, use GitHub's reporting feature. On the offending comment, issue, or PR, click **…** in the top-right corner and choose **Report content**; to report a user, choose **Block or report** on their profile. When you choose to report to this repository's maintainers, the maintainers are notified.

The maintainers will look into reports as soon as possible and keep the reporter's identity confidential. Depending on the case, they may remove or edit the content in question, issue a warning, or temporarily or permanently ban the person from taking part in the project.
